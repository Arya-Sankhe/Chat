Send the Klui email

Thank you for sending this to your attendees. It takes about two minutes from your own Gmail or Outlook. Nothing to install.

  1. Copy the email.
  2. Start a new email in Gmail or Outlook, click into the message area, and paste with Ctrl + V (⌘ + V on a Mac).
  3. Add the subject. Here's one idea, but it's only a suggestion. Feel free to write whatever you like.
  4. Put the attendees in Bcc, not in To, so nobody sees everyone else's address. Put your own address in To.
  5. Send it to yourself first and check it looks right, then send it to everyone.
