Send the Klui email

Thank you for sending this to your attendees. It takes about two minutes from your own Gmail or Outlook. Nothing to install.

  1. Copy the email.
  2. Start a new email in Gmail or Outlook, click into the message area, and paste with Ctrl + V (⌘ + V on a Mac). The pictures and the "Claim free Pro" button come along with it.
  3. Add the subject.
  4. Put the attendees in Bcc, not in To, so nobody sees everyone else's address. Put your own address in To.
  5. Send it to yourself first and check it looks right, then send it to everyone.
Gmail lets a personal account send to about 500 people a day (2,000 for a school or work Google account). For a bigger list, send it in a few batches.
Using Mailchimp, Brevo or another mailing tool instead?

Download the email file and pick the "paste or import your own HTML" option when you create the campaign. On phones it looks a little better that way.
