Privacy policy

This notice describes how ARCSCALE Group (FZC) (“Klui”, “we”) handles personal data when you use Klui. Related: Terms of use, Cookie policy, Subprocessors.

1. Who we are

ARCSCALE Group (FZC), Sharjah Research Technology and Innovation Park, Block B, Office B59-076, Sharjah, United Arab Emirates (licence 11814). Contact: privacy@klui.ai.

2. What we collect

  • Account. Google sign-in gives us your email and display name. We do not collect a date of birth, phone number, or password of our own.
  • Your content. Chats, uploads, project files, study material, memory you turn on, and reports you submit.
  • Voice. If you use voice input, audio is sent to a speech-to-text provider. Klui does not keep the audio file.
  • Billing. Plan, status, provider, and period dates. Card details stay with Mamo or Ziina. We do not vault cards.
  • Usage. Weekly API-credit usage so we can apply plan limits. We do not run ad analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • Device settings. Appearance and similar preferences on this device after you accept the storage notice (native apps write them immediately).
  • Desktop (Windows beta). If you use Klui Anything, screen or voice you choose to share can be sent so the desktop features work.

We do not collect GPS from the device. Camera photos you upload can contain location in EXIF; we strip EXIF/GPS before we keep those images. We do not collect video or calendar data.

3. How we use it

To run the product: sign you in, generate replies, search the web when you ask, store files, meter usage, take payment, handle reports, and delete or export your account when you ask. Staff can see account email, plan, payments, and content in a report, to operate Klui and handle abuse.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share it for cross-context advertising. We honor Global Privacy Control as the default for everyone, because there is no ad-tech to turn off. See /.well-known/gpc.json.

4. Models and training

We send prompts, conversation history, attachments the model needs, and tool results to OpenRouter, which routes them to model hosts. We do not route requests to providers OpenRouter classifies as ones that “may use prompts for training.” Some providers “may retain prompts, but do not use them for training” (abuse, legal, or operational logging). That is allowed. We do not promise zero retention. OpenRouter’s labels are their catalog, not a warranty of each host’s law.

Klui does not train its own models on your chats.

5. Memory

Memory is off until you turn it on. It starts with messages you send after that. Klui does not build memory from AI replies, uploaded files, images, tool results, generated documents, or temporary chats. You can edit or clear it in Settings.

6. Search

If a turn uses web search, the query and fetched page text go to a search provider (SearXNG and/or Jina and/or Brave) so we can answer you. We do not keep a shared search-query cache and we do not share raw queries across users. There is no daily search-count cap; search cost is part of weekly usage.

7. Files

Uploads and generated images live on Cloudflare R2. Uploaded photos are stored without EXIF/GPS. Generated illustrations are cartoon explainers, not photoreal people. We do not currently stamp C2PA on those files.

8. How long we keep it

Until you delete the conversation, file, or account. Account deletion is immediate. Unused pending uploads can be cleaned up after a short grace period. We do not currently run a 12-month inactivity email.

9. Your rights

Download my data (Settings → Account) is a JSON export of chats, file metadata, memory, and billing metadata. Delete account removes the account and data we have keys for. For other requests, email privacy@klui.ai. We do not currently send those replies by automated mail.

10. Children

Klui is 18+. We do not knowingly keep accounts for anyone under 18.

11. Security

Access to product data is through signed-in APIs and our database. Files use private object storage. This is not a claim that we have a pentest program, a DPO, or encryption we have not built. Report a problem to privacy@klui.ai.

12. Changes

We may update this page and the date at the top. We do not currently email a notice.
