Cookie policy

Klui does not run advertising or use non-essential tracking. Details: Privacy policy.

What we store on your device

  • Sign-in. Necessary session storage keeps you signed in and protects your account. It lasts until it expires, you sign out, or you clear the site's data.
  • Settings. Local storage remembers appearance and similar choices until you clear Klui's site data in your browser settings.

Third-party loads

  • Google Identity loads when you open sign-in, not on first paint of the chat app.
  • Google Fonts provide some chat-app typefaces. This marketing site uses first-party fonts.
  • Some chat libraries load from jsDelivr after the app starts. These services receive ordinary request information such as your IP address.

What we do not do

No advertising cookies, advertising SDKs, cross-site profiling, or analytics cookies. Because Klui does not use optional tracking, there are no tracking categories to accept or reject.
