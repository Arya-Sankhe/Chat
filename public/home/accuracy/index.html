Can Klui make mistakes?

Yes. Klui can sometimes give an answer that is incorrect, incomplete, or out of date—even when it sounds confident.

Why can answers be wrong?

Klui uses generative AI models. They produce answers from patterns and the context available to them, but they do not automatically know whether every statement is true or current.

How should I check an important answer?

Check the original source before relying on an answer for an important decision. You can also ask Klui to explain its reasoning or find current sources.

What if an answer includes sources?

Open the sources and make sure they support the answer. A source may contain context or details that are not included in Klui's summary.

Can I use Klui for medical, legal, or financial decisions?

Klui can help you understand a subject and prepare questions, but it should not be your only source for a high-stakes decision. Confirm important guidance with a qualified professional. In an emergency, contact your local emergency service.

How can I get a better answer?

Give relevant context, say when information needs to be current, ask follow-up questions, and request sources when they would help.

How can I report a problem?

Use the feedback or report control on the response. Reports help us investigate answers that are incorrect, unsafe, or otherwise unhelpful.
