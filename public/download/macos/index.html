Klui Anything for Mac

Let’s put Klui on your Mac.

The Mac beta is invite-only for now. Open the link you were sent to download it.

Checking your invite…

Apple silicon Mac (M1 or newer) · macOS 14 Sonoma or newer

  1. Install. Open the downloaded file and drag KluiAnything into Applications.
  2. Open it once by hand. This early beta isn’t verified by Apple yet, so macOS will refuse the first time. Click Done, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to KluiAnything. Or paste this in Terminal: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/KluiAnything.app
  3. Sign in with Google using the account you use on Klui. Your plan and weekly usage come with you. If macOS ever asks to let KluiAnything use your keychain, enter your Mac password and click Always Allow.
  4. Give it permissions. Turn on Accessibility and Screen Recording when Klui asks (microphone too if you want to talk). It relaunches once.
  5. Say hi. Double-tap ⌃ from any app and ask something, or hold ⌃ ⌥ and talk.
A little beta note

This is a very early build, so expect rough edges. Klui only looks at your screen when you ask. Letting it click and type for you is the newest part; if it gets something wrong, tell whoever sent you this link. Usage comes from the same weekly Klui allowance as the website.
